Python · Power BI · Data validation · Financial modeling

Read case study

Business question

Where should packaging recyclability efforts focus across eight global plants?

Approach

Compared historic packaging quantities with transformed records in Python and integrated multi-year packaging and sales data in Power BI dashboards to track recyclability KPIs. Contributed to a 10-year energy-efficiency financial model.

Key finding

Secondary packaging emerged as the main non-recyclable waste driver.

Recommendation

Use the plant-level dashboards to prioritize packaging improvements and evaluate energy-efficiency investment scenarios.

Scope & limitations

The $50,000 investment scenario projected a five-year payback and 106.5% ROI. These are modeled outcomes, not realized savings. The attached validation notebook is a documented cleanup of the original method; source datasets are not included and original-data results have not been reproduced.

Social impact

Understanding where food assistance is needed.

Data Science Alliance · 2023
USD academic consulting project

9 datasetsmerged and cleaned for analysis

Non-white renter population and rent burden were the strongest predictors of meal demand. Ninety percent of the highest-need ZIP codes had above-average poverty and median incomes about $10,000 below the city median.

SQL · Python · Regression · Hypothesis testing

Read case study

Business question

Which local characteristics are associated with food bank meal demand?

Approach

Merged multi-year demographic, socioeconomic, and food bank datasets using SQL and Python. Applied regression and hypothesis testing.

Key finding

Non-white renter population and rent burden were the strongest predictors of meal demand. Ninety percent of the highest-need ZIP codes had above-average poverty and median incomes about $10,000 below the city median.

Recommendation

Use geographic need indicators to inform resource allocation.

Scope & limitations

The findings describe associations, not causal effects.
